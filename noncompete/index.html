Why you are signing. In exchange for your engagement or continued engagement with the Company, and the access it gives you to the confidential information above, you agree to the terms below.

Time covered. The non-compete and non-solicitation below apply for the entire time you are engaged with the Company, starting on the date above, and for twelve (12) months after your last day with the Company, no matter how or by whom the engagement ends. The confidentiality obligation has no end date.

Non-compete. During the time covered, you will not own, operate, work for, or provide services to any business that designs, prints, or sells custom labels, stickers, or packaging graphics for cannabis or hemp products within the State of Michigan. This does not stop you from using your general skills in unrelated work, or from holding two percent (2%) or less of a publicly traded company.

Non-solicitation. During the time covered, you will not solicit, serve, or divert any customer or prospect of the Company that you dealt with or learned about during your last twelve (12) months with the Company, and you will not recruit or hire away any of the Company's employees or contractors.

Confidential information. You will keep the Company's confidential information strictly confidential during and after your engagement, use it only for the Company's work, and return or destroy all Company materials, files, and artwork when your engagement ends or on request.

Your protected rights. Nothing in this Agreement stops you from discussing wages or working conditions with co-workers, from reporting a possible violation of law to a government agency, or from cooperating with one. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), you will not be held liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

Reasonableness; remedies. You agree these restrictions are reasonable in time, area, and scope and protect the Company's legitimate business interests under Michigan law (MCL 445.774a). If a court finds any restriction too broad, it may narrow it and enforce it as narrowed. Because a breach would cause harm that money alone may not repair, the Company may seek an injunction without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy. If you breach, the twelve (12) months are extended by the length of the breach.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Michigan, with venue in Washtenaw County. It does not by itself create employment or a guarantee of continued engagement. It may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic or photographed signature, and each Party keeps a signed copy.
