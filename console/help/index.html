How the order tracker works staff guide

One timeline per job, from the Square invoice to pickup. The client watches it on their phone, Monday follows it, and the team gets nudged when something sits too long.

The one habit that makes it all work

Every job has a code from its Square invoice number, like 247-XL. Put that code in the Square invoice title and in the Monday item name. That is how the tracker, Monday and the client's link all find the same job.

If an order has no Monday item with its code, you get a Monday notification saying so. Add the code to the item name and it links itself within 10 minutes.

Every stage change emails the client a short update with a link to their timeline. Monday and the tracker sync both ways every 10 minutes and only move forward, so a Monday label that is behind never drags an order back.

The console is the exception: a tap there can move an order backward to correct a mistake.

Emails you will get

  • Proof approved. The client tapped Approve. Print it.
  • Needs a push. An order sat in Proof sent more than 24 hours or in Printing more than 72 hours. Sent at most once a day per order. The client is never nudged, only us.
  • Week in review. Monday 7 am: new orders, stage changes, open orders by stage, stale orders, and what people asked the app's chatbot.
  • Monday notification. A tracker order has no Monday item. Put the job code in the item name.

What the client sees

Their emails link to a timeline page showing the stages, the current message and the due date. In the app they can open Track my order from the top bar or the Connect tab, or ask the chatbot "Where's my order?" with their code and email.

Each Monday item has two link columns: Tracker opens that order in the console, Client link opens exactly what the client sees.
