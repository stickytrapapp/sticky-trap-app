What we collect

When you request a quote, place an order, or contact us, we collect the information needed to do the work: your name, company, email address, phone number, shipping or pick-up details, your artwork files, and the details of your order. Our app also keeps a small amount of anonymous usage data (for example which menu items are viewed) to improve the app.

How we use it

To quote, proof, produce, deliver and invoice your order; to send you order status updates by email and, if you choose, by text message; to answer your questions; and to keep records we are required to keep for tax and accounting. We do not sell your information.

Text messages (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number when placing an order, you agree to receive order status text messages from The Sticky Trap (quote sent, proof ready for approval, approved, printing, ready for pickup, shipped). Message frequency varies by order, typically 3 to 8 messages per order. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts; your order updates continue by email. Reply HELP for help, or call or text 734 460 3845. You can also switch text updates on or off at any time from your order tracker page.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run the shop: our payment processor (Square) for invoices and card payments, our email and messaging providers (Google, Twilio) to deliver updates, and our shipping carrier for deliveries. Each receives only what it needs for that job. We share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing thestickytrap@gmail.com. We keep order records as long as the law requires for business and tax purposes.

Security

Order records live in access-controlled systems. Card details are handled by Square and never stored by us.
