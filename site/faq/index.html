FAQ

Straight answers.

The things people ask before their first order. Anything else: text 734 460 3845.

Do you price match?

Found a better price? Send us the quote and we'll see what we can do.

Do I need to bring artwork?

No. We're a design house — bring a finished file or just an idea, and we'll design it, dial in the materials, and print it in-house.

Will you help me find the best options for my art?

Yes — we don't just run your file. Send whatever you've got and, if you want, we add the finishing that makes it land, plus the right material call. Curated per project, quoted with your order.

Can I get a custom shape?

Yes — Custom Dielines. We build a bespoke cut path to any outline you want, not just a square or circle. Quoted per project.

What are pre-press / set-up charges?

Pre-press makes your art print-ready — set-up, color, cut paths, bleeds, proofing. We assess it per design once we see your files, before anything prints. Print-ready vector files keep it minimal. Anything beyond that we quote up front, before we start.

Can I send AI art?

Yes — most of what we get now is ChatGPT. Send the biggest original PNG and keep the text out of the image; we take it from there: real type, clean vector, set up for your exact material and texture. *Per file, for art that follows the tips. Photoreal renders and heavier fixes get one written quote before we start.

9 tips before you generate
  1. Keep text out of the image. Send the exact wording separately — we set it in real type, spelled right.
  2. Ask for flat, bold, hard-edged art. Solid colors, clean outlines. No gradients, glow, noise or photoreal.
  3. Plain or transparent background. No frame, shadow or jar mockup baked in — we add the texture.
  4. Download the biggest original. Save the PNG the tool gives you. No screenshots, no texting it over.
  5. Match the sticker's shape. Square for a lid or bottom, wide for a wrap — ask the tool for that ratio.
  6. Leave breathing room. Nothing important touching the edges. We crop to the cut line.
  7. One image per sticker, named. lid.png, wrap.png, bottom.png — or just tell us which is which.
  8. Tell us what pops. Which parts get gloss or raise? ‘The logo and the strain name’ is plenty.
  9. Type out the required text. Strain, weight, warnings, license line — as text in the email, not in the art.
