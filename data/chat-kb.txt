Menu tab: open a product, set the quantity, tap a price to add it to the basket. The basket can Email my order, Text order, Copy text, or "Upload art & get your quote" (name, email, phone, art files up to ~20 MB, deadline). Bigger files: email them.

A CARD ORDER is different: when a customer pays by card in the basket, the card is charged when the order is placed, not when the proof is approved. If they cancel before we print, we refund what they paid less any design, pre-press or proofing already done; once it is on the press it cannot be cancelled.
Minimum order: $50 per order. Quantities step by 5 in the app, from 5 up to 1,000; more than 1,000 is a custom quote (call us).
Volume price breaks on the per-piece menu price: 500+ pcs 5% off, 750+ pcs 7% off, 1,000+ pcs 10% off. Under 500 pcs pays the menu price.

== Texture levels (every product, every material) ==
The menu calls the finish tiers "textures"; the number is the layers of UV gloss.
Flat -- full-color print, no gloss or emboss (texture 0; older menus and invoices call it "Base").
Spot Gloss UV -- texture 1: one layer, a glossy raised accent on select areas.
Standard Embossing -- texture 2: two layers of built-up gloss/emboss for real texture.
Pro -- texture 3: three layers, full dimensional emboss, the premium menu tier.
Flex (Ridge, Summit, Peak, Apex, Zenith) -- 4 to 8 layers, custom quoted above Pro; never a menu price.
HoloBrite -- a white-ink underbase under the art on Holographic or Gold for true, razor-sharp color while the metal still frames it. Listed as its own per-piece price where offered.

Materials: White Vinyl (6 mil), Holographic (6 mil rainbow, bubble-free -- older menus say "Holographic BF" / "Holo BF"), Gold (4 mil, bubble-free; older menus say "Gold BF"). Tip Bands use White Vinyl or a non-bubble-free Holographic film.

9 tips before you generate
Keep text out of the image. Send the exact wording separately — we set it in real type, spelled right.
Ask for flat, bold, hard-edged art. Solid colors, clean outlines. No gradients, glow, noise or photoreal.
Plain or transparent background. No frame, shadow or jar mockup baked in — we add the texture.
Download the biggest original. Save the PNG the tool gives you. No screenshots, no texting it over.
Match the sticker's shape. Square for a lid or bottom, wide for a wrap — ask the tool for that ratio.
Leave breathing room. Nothing important touching the edges. We crop to the cut line.
One image per sticker, named. lid.png, wrap.png, bottom.png — or just tell us which is which.
Tell us what pops. Which parts get gloss or raise? ‘The logo and the strain name’ is plenty.
Type out the required text. Strain, weight, warnings, license line — as text in the email, not in the art.
