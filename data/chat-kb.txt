== Ordering in the app ==
Menu tab: open a product, set the quantity, tap a price to add it to the basket. The basket can Email my order, Text order, Copy text, or "Upload art & get your quote" (name, email, phone, art files up to ~20 MB, deadline). Bigger files: email them.
Nothing is charged in the app: we proof and quote first, and nothing is charged until the customer approves. The "Start a project -- get a quote" button on the Connect tab goes to the same quote form.
Minimum order: 50 pieces per item. Quantities step by 5 in the app, up to 1,000; more than 1,000 is a custom quote (call us).
Volume price breaks on the per-piece menu price: 250+ pcs 3% off, 500+ pcs 6% off, 1,000 pcs 10% off. Under 250 pcs pays the menu price. Setup / pre-press is quoted separately.
Example: 3" Slap, White Vinyl, Base is $0.45 each -> 100 pcs = $45.00; 500 pcs = 500 x $0.45 x 0.94 = $211.50.

== Finish tiers (every product, every material) ==
Base -- flat print, no gloss or emboss.
Spot Gloss UV -- 1 layer: a glossy raised accent on select areas.
Standard Embossing -- 2 layers of built-up gloss/emboss for real texture.
Pro -- 3 layers: full dimensional emboss, the premium menu tier.
Flex (Ridge, Summit, Peak, Apex, Zenith) -- 4 to 8 layers, custom quoted above Pro; never a menu price.
HoloBrite -- a white-ink underbase under the art on Holographic BF or Gold BF for true, razor-sharp color while the metal still frames it. Listed as its own per-piece price where offered.

Materials & Finishes

HoloBrite™

Custom Embossing
A raised, dimensional texture you can actually feel. This is where the gloss gets layered and built up.
How We Work
Getting to Print

Proofing
We proof every file with you before we print your order — shared with you as photos and video by text or email, whichever you prefer — so you sign off on exactly what prints, no surprises.
Common Questions
Do you price match?
Found a better price? Send us the quote and we'll see what we can do.
Do I need to bring artwork?
No. We're a design house — bring a finished file or just an idea, and we'll design it, dial in the materials, and print it in-house.
Will you help me find the best options for my art?
Yes — we don't just run your file. Send whatever you've got and, if you want, we add the finishing that makes it land, plus the right material call. Curated per project, quoted with your order.

What are pre-press / set-up charges?
Pre-press makes your art print-ready — resolution, color, cut paths, bleeds, proofing. Real hands-on work, so shops charge for it. Send print-ready files and there's often little or none; if not, we quote it up front.
