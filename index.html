What do the finish tiers mean?

Base — flat print, no gloss or emboss.

Spot Gloss UV — 1 layer: a glossy raised accent on select areas.

Standard — 2 layers of built-up gloss/emboss for real texture.

Pro — 3 layers: full dimensional emboss, our premium menu tier.

Flex (Ridge→Zenith) — 4–8 layers, custom quoted above Pro.

Effects

Spot Gloss UV

A high-gloss coating on a select area, so whatever you want to stand out catches the light — ideal for extreme, detailed accents.

HoloBrite™

A white-ink base under the art on holographic or gold stock for true, razor-sharp color while the metal still frames it.

Enquire — custom quoted

Custom Embossing

A raised, dimensional texture you can actually feel — genuine depth and relief that gives logos and type real presence, and reads premium by touch. This is where the gloss gets layered and built up — finished with a sharp, braille-like edge or a softer, rounded feel. It's part of our custom curated process: we work with you to get the right look and feel for your project.

Enquire — custom quoted

Getting to Print

It all starts with your art files. Print-ready files get you to print fastest — and we'll assess yours free. If they're not print-ready, we'll explain why and give you a menu of options. The good news: we provide just about any service needed to still get you to print — and we'll walk you through it. Please email them to us to get started.

Proofing

We proof every file with you before we print your order — shared with you as photos and video by text or email, whichever you prefer — so you sign off on exactly what prints, no surprises.

Curation

Curation, not just printing. We don't just run your file — if requested, we add the finishing that makes it land, plus the right material call. Curated per project and quoted with your order.

Common Questions

Do I need to bring artwork?

No. We're a design house — bring a finished file or just an idea, and we'll design it, dial in the materials, and print it in-house.

Can I get a custom shape?

Yes — Custom Dielines. We build a bespoke cut path to any outline you want, not just a square or circle. Quoted per project.

What are pre-press / set-up charges — and why do shops charge them?

Pre-press is the work that turns your art into a production-ready file — checking resolution and color, building cut paths and bleeds, separating layers, and proofing. Shops charge for it because it's real hands-on time before a single piece prints. Send print-ready files and there's often little or none; if yours need work, we will provide pricing and options up front for you.
