Materials & Finishes

Substrate

Our materials. We run only stocks we'd put our name on — if it won't print to that standard, we don't offer it. Other premium materials available on request.

White Vinyl

6 mil premium white vinyl — durable, with crisp, clean color.

Rainbow Holographic BF

6 mil rainbow holographic — bubble-free (BF). Lays dead-flat, applies clean.

Gold BF

4 mil premium gold — warm metallic shine, bubble-free; makes gold-forward art read high-end.

ClearBy quote

Premium glass-clear stock — art reads like it's floating in the material. A niche specialty, priced per project.

Rainbow HolographicTip bands & tabs

6 mil rainbow holographic (not bubble-free) — our solution for tip bands, tamper tabs & small pieces; helps them stick.

Effects

Spot Gloss UV

A high-gloss coating on a select area, so whatever you want to stand out catches the light — ideal for extreme, detailed accents.

HoloBrite™

A white-ink base under the art on holographic or gold stock for true, razor-sharp color while the metal still frames it.

Custom Embossing

A raised, dimensional texture you can actually feel. This is where the gloss gets layered and built up.

How We Work

Getting to Print

Print-ready art files get you to print fastest — we'll assess yours free. Not there yet? We provide whatever's needed to get you there, and walk you through it. Email them to us to start.

Proofing

We proof every file with you before we print your order — shared with you as photos and video by text or email, whichever you prefer — so you sign off on exactly what prints, no surprises.

Common Questions

Do you price match?

Found a better price? Send us the quote and we'll see what we can do.

Do I need to bring artwork?

No. We're a design house — bring a finished file or just an idea, and we'll design it, dial in the materials, and print it in-house.

Will you help me find the best options for my art?

Yes — we don't just run your file. Send whatever you've got and, if you want, we add the finishing that makes it land, plus the right material call. Curated per project, quoted with your order.

Can I get a custom shape?

Yes — Custom Dielines. We build a bespoke cut path to any outline you want, not just a square or circle. Quoted per project.

What are pre-press / set-up charges?

Pre-press makes your art print-ready — resolution, color, cut paths, bleeds, proofing. Real hands-on work, so shops charge for it. Send print-ready files and there's often little or none; if not, we quote it up front.
